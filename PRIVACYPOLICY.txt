PRIVACY POLICY FOR PATIENTCOPILOT.IN

Effective Date: 01 April 2026
Version: 1.1 (Last updated: 07 September 2026)

This Privacy Policy explains how PatientCopilot ("Platform"), operated by
Bijeesh Balakrishnan ("we", "us", "Operator"), collects, uses, stores, shares,
and protects your personal information. It forms an integral part of, and
should be read together with, our User Agreement.

We act as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act,
2023 (DPDP Act) of India.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Account data
  Name, email address, phone number, and login credentials.

Health Data (sensitive)
  Medical records, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, bills, consultation
  records, and related documents you upload.

Photos you capture or upload
  Images of documents you scan or attach, taken with your camera or chosen
  from your photo library.

Voice recordings
  Voice notes you record for appointments or reminders, if you use that
  feature.

Family member data
  Information you add about family members or other people in your care.

Usage and device data
  App interactions, device type and operating system, a push-notification
  token and device identifier (so we can deliver notifications to your
  device), and diagnostic/crash reports generated when something goes
  wrong in the app. These crash reports are handled entirely by our own
  backend — we do not use a third-party analytics or crash-reporting
  vendor.

We collect only the data necessary for the purposes described below (data
minimization).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

We use your data strictly to:

- Store and organize your extracted and de-identified medical records
- Provide health insights, reminders, and analytics
- Facilitate sharing of records with people you authorize
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Platform
- Comply with legal obligations

AI processing. To generate health insights and conversational chat
responses, we use AI models from third-party providers, currently Google
Vertex AI/Gemini, OpenAI, and Anthropic Claude. Before any Health Data is
sent to any of these providers, direct identifiers — your name and your
family members' names — are replaced with a stable pseudonym; the model
never sees your real identity, and the real name is restored only in the
answer shown back to you. For reliability, requests may be routed to
whichever of these providers is available at that moment, rather than
depending on a single vendor. These providers do not use this data to
train their own models and process it solely to generate the response you
requested.

We never sell your data. We never use your Health Data for advertising or
marketing. We never use your Health Data to train artificial intelligence
or machine learning models.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. LEGAL BASIS / CONSENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

We process your Personal Data on the basis of your free, specific,
informed, and unambiguous consent, as required under the DPDP Act. You may
withdraw consent at any time (see Section 8).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HEALTH DATA & SENSITIVE INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

We treat Health Data as highly sensitive. It is:

- Encrypted in transit and at rest
- Accessible only on a role-based, need-to-know basis
- Never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI/ML
  models
- Pseudonymized (direct identifiers removed or replaced) before being sent
  to any AI provider for processing

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CHILDREN'S DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

For data relating to minors, we require verifiable parental or guardian
consent and take reasonable steps to verify it. We do not track, profile,
behaviorally monitor, or direct targeted advertising at children, and we
do not process children's data in any manner likely to cause harm.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW WE SHARE DATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

We do not share your data except:

- With your explicit consent
- With service providers / sub-processors bound by confidentiality and
  data-protection obligations equivalent to ours, on a need-to-know and,
  where feasible, de-identified basis
- With our AI service providers (Google Vertex AI/Gemini, OpenAI, and
  Anthropic Claude), to process pseudonymized Health Data solely to
  generate AI-based insights and chat responses. These providers are
  contractually prohibited from using this data to train their models.
- Where required by law

Categories of sub-processors include Google Cloud Run, Google Cloud
Storage, Google Vertex AI, OpenAI, and Anthropic Claude.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. DATA STORAGE & CROSS-BORDER TRANSFER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your data is primarily stored on infrastructure located in India (Mumbai).
Where data is stored or processed outside India, or by sub-processors,
such transfers are carried out in accordance with the DPDP Act and
applicable law.

Some of our AI service providers (including OpenAI and Anthropic Claude)
may process pseudonymized data on infrastructure located outside India.
Only pseudonymized Health Data — with direct identifiers removed — is
transmitted to these providers, and such transfers are carried out in
accordance with the DPDP Act and applicable law.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. YOUR RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

- Access your data
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
- Request erasure / deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent at any time
- Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in case of death or
  incapacity
- Grievance redressal

To exercise these rights, use the in-app controls or contact our
Grievance Officer (Section 11).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly
within the app under Settings -> Delete Account. A web-based deletion
request is also available at https://patientcopilot.in/delete-account.

Upon deletion, your data is erased except where retention is required by
law.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. DATA RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described,
or as required by law. When no longer needed, data is securely deleted.
